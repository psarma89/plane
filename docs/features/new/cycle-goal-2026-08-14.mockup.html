Three decisions that this drawing forces.

1. An empty goal renders nothing. It does not render a placeholder. A placeholder in every row of a list is noise, and most cycles will never carry a goal.

2. A long goal truncates on one line with an ellipsis. It does not wrap. A row that grows to three lines breaks the rhythm of the list.

3. The goal reads as text-tertiary, below the cycle name. The name stays the subject of the row.
